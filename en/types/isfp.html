Cognitive Function Stack

Dominant Function
Fi
Introverted Feeling (Fi)
Guided by inner values and true feelings, loyal to self — judging “does this feel right to my heart”.
Auxiliary Function
Se
Extraverted Sensing (Se)
Lives in the moment, keenly absorbs real-time details and sensory experience, reacts fast and adapts on the fly.
Tertiary Function

Organizes and executes efficiently on the outside world, speaking through standards, rules and results — inclined to “get it done”.

Key Traits

  • Low-key yet opinionated
  • Expresses self through action, not words
  • Gentle and inclusive, with clear boundaries
