ENFP · Campaigner
Diplomats (NF)

Ne-dominant: enthusiastic, curious and imaginative, endlessly interested in people and new things. Fi-auxiliary picks out what truly moves you from all the possibilities — a natural fit for creative and people-connecting work.

Cognitive Function Stack

Dominant Function
Ne
Extraverted Intuition (Ne)
Continuously branches out new ideas and possibilities, enjoys brainstorming, making connections and trying new paths.
Auxiliary Function
Fi
Introverted Feeling (Fi)
Guided by inner values and true feelings, loyal to self — judging “does this feel right to my heart”.
Tertiary Function
Te
Extraverted Thinking (Te)
Organizes and executes efficiently on the outside world, speaking through standards, rules and results — inclined to “get it done”.
Inferior Function
Si
Introverted Sensing (Si)
Consolidates experience and details, relies on familiar routines and memory, seeking stability and predictability.

Key Traits

  • Ne-dominant: enthusiastic, curious, endlessly imaginative
  • Fi-auxiliary: picks out what truly moves you from endless possibilities
  • Deeply interested in people and new things
  • A natural fit for creative and people-connecting work
  • Highly infectious, gets people around you excited
