Privacy Policy

We respect and protect your privacy. This page explains how this site handles personal data during your visit and the test, the use of cookies and local storage, third-party advertising services, and the rights you have.

Effective date: 2026-09-13 · Last updated: 2026-09-13

1. What Data We Collect

Test answers are neither stored nor uploaded. All tests on this website (MBTI Cognitive Function Test) are computed entirely in your browser. Your answers, scores and type results are never sent to any server, and we cannot view or retain your test records.

No registration, no login, no personal data collection. This site does not require an account and does not proactively collect identifiable personal information such as your name, email, phone number or ID number.

The hosting provider and advertising partners (see third-party services below) may record log data necessary for operation, such as IP address, browser type, access time and page URL, for website security, analytics and ad delivery.

2. Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses browser localStorage to save the following two kinds of information, both of which remain on your device and are never uploaded to any server:

  • Test progress: lets you resume an unfinished test after refreshing or leaving the page (can be cleared manually where supported);
  • Theme preference: remembers the light / dark display mode you chose and applies it automatically on your next visit.

You can clear local storage data at any time through your browser settings. Doing so only affects the progress and appearance preferences above and does not affect any other site functionality.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdSense)

This site displays third-party ads through Google AdSense to keep running. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show more relevant ads based on your browsing history on other websites (interest-based advertising).

  • Third-party / DART cookies: Google and its advertising partners may serve DART cookies or third-party cookies to display ads on their respective websites.
  • Personalized ad settings: You can view, manage or opt out of Google's personalized ads at any time at https://adssettings.google.com.
  • Opt-out options: You can also visit www.aboutads.info to learn about and opt out of some third-party ad networks, or disable third-party cookies in your browser settings.

The use of third-party cookies and data is governed by the privacy policies of the respective ad providers (such as Google); this site is not responsible for their processing practices. We recommend that you also read Google's privacy policy to understand its data practices.

4. Data Security

Since this site does not store your test answers or personal information, your risk of personal data exposure is extremely low. For access logs recorded at the server and hosting level, we apply reasonable security measures (such as access control and minimized retention) to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure or alteration.

Please note: no method of transmission or storage over the internet can guarantee absolute security. We make every reasonable effort to protect your data.

5. Your Rights

  • Access and correction: Since we do not keep your personal information, there is no need to request access or correction from us; local data on your device can be viewed and cleared at any time in your browser settings.
  • Deletion: You can clear all local storage data for this site (test progress, theme preference, etc.) through your browser settings.
  • Opting out of personalized ads: You can opt out of interest-based personalized ads via Google Ad Settings, or disable third-party cookies in your browser.
  • Submitting requests: If you have any questions or requests regarding your personal data, please contact us through the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Policy Updates & Contact

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time to reflect legal requirements, site features or changes to third-party services. Updated versions will be published on this page, and the effective date at the top of the page will be updated accordingly. For significant changes, we will notify users in a prominent place on the site.
