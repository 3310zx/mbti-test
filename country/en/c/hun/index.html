Strengths and soft spots

Strongest: Safety · 73

Homicides per 100,000 people: 0.72 per 100k (2023). That is rank 15 across the 53 countries reporting this indicator, and it is the main source of Hungary's overall score.

Weakest: Health · 26

Life expectancy: 76.7 yrs (2024). It ranks 42 here, so this is the trade-off you accept when you pick Hungary.

Frequently asked questions

Where does Hungary rank overall among the 55 countries?

With all six dimensions weighted equally, Hungary scores 53.4 and ranks 23 of 55. Safety comes in at 73 (a strong point, rank 15) and Health at 26 (clearly weak, rank 42). These are percentiles of the candidate set, not absolute verdicts.

How expensive is Hungary, and what do people earn there?

Cost of living index (price level): 52.0 on the price level index (US=100, 2025); GDP per capita (PPP): 49,838 intl.$ (2025). Both are macro averages: the price level index compares PPP conversion factors with market exchange rates and ignores individual spending patterns, while GDP per capita in PPP terms measures output rather than take-home pay.

Can the data alone tell me whether to move to Hungary?

No. These are national statistical averages and cannot capture differences between cities and neighbourhoods, visa and tax rules, language and access to public services, or your own profession and family situation. Use them for shortlisting and cross-country comparison, and consult a licensed professional for the actual move, visa and tax arrangements.
