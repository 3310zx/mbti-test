Strengths and soft spots

Strongest: Cost of living · 98

Cost of living index (price level): 23.1 on the price level index (US=100, 2025). That is rank 2 across the 55 countries reporting this indicator, and it is the main source of India's overall score.

Weakest: Society & digital · 2

Urban population share: 35.7% (2025). It ranks 55 here, so this is the trade-off you accept when you pick India.

Frequently asked questions

Where does India rank overall among the 55 countries?

With all six dimensions weighted equally, India scores 29.7 and ranks 54 of 55. Cost of living comes in at 98 (a strong point, rank 2) and Society & digital at 2 (clearly weak, rank 55). These are percentiles of the candidate set, not absolute verdicts.

How expensive is India, and what do people earn there?

Cost of living index (price level): 23.1 on the price level index (US=100, 2025); GDP per capita (PPP): 11,748 intl.$ (2025). Both are macro averages: the price level index compares PPP conversion factors with market exchange rates and ignores individual spending patterns, while GDP per capita in PPP terms measures output rather than take-home pay.

Can the data alone tell me whether to move to India?

No. These are national statistical averages and cannot capture differences between cities and neighbourhoods, visa and tax rules, language and access to public services, or your own profession and family situation. Use them for shortlisting and cross-country comparison, and consult a licensed professional for the actual move, visa and tax arrangements.
