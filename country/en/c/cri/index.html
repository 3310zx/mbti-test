Strengths and soft spots

Strongest: Environment · 66

Population density: 101.5 /km² (2024). That is rank 13 across the 55 countries reporting this indicator, and it is the main source of Costa Rica's overall score.

Weakest: Safety · 8

Homicides per 100,000 people: 17.75 per 100k (2023). It ranks 49 here, so this is the trade-off you accept when you pick Costa Rica.

Healthcare and eco-tourism are mature, and prices run above the neighbouring countries.

Frequently asked questions

Where does Costa Rica rank overall among the 55 countries?

With all six dimensions weighted equally, Costa Rica scores 38.5 and ranks 46 of 55. Environment comes in at 66 (mid-pack, rank 13) and Safety at 8 (clearly weak, rank 49). These are percentiles of the candidate set, not absolute verdicts.

How expensive is Costa Rica, and what do people earn there?

Cost of living index (price level): 59.1 on the price level index (US=100, 2025); GDP per capita (PPP): 33,815 intl.$ (2025). Both are macro averages: the price level index compares PPP conversion factors with market exchange rates and ignores individual spending patterns, while GDP per capita in PPP terms measures output rather than take-home pay.

Can the data alone tell me whether to move to Costa Rica?

No. These are national statistical averages and cannot capture differences between cities and neighbourhoods, visa and tax rules, language and access to public services, or your own profession and family situation. Use them for shortlisting and cross-country comparison, and consult a licensed professional for the actual move, visa and tax arrangements.
