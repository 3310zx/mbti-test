Frequently asked questions

Where does United States rank overall among the 55 countries?

With all six dimensions weighted equally, United States scores 49.7 and ranks 33 of 55. Earning power comes in at 80 (a strong point, rank 6) and Cost of living at 6 (clearly weak, rank 52). These are percentiles of the candidate set, not absolute verdicts.

How expensive is United States, and what do people earn there?

Cost of living index (price level): 100.0 on the price level index (US=100, 2025); GDP per capita (PPP): 90,027 intl.$ (2025). Both are macro averages: the price level index compares PPP conversion factors with market exchange rates and ignores individual spending patterns, while GDP per capita in PPP terms measures output rather than take-home pay.

Can the data alone tell me whether to move to United States?

No. These are national statistical averages and cannot capture differences between cities and neighbourhoods, visa and tax rules, language and access to public services, or your own profession and family situation. Use them for shortlisting and cross-country comparison, and consult a licensed professional for the actual move, visa and tax arrangements.
